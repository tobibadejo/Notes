Question One
**a)** Briefly explain the role of embedded systems in modern autonomous vehicles, industrial robotics, and edge AI applications with at least one specific example for each. **(5 marks)**

ANS: Embedded systems are specialized computer systems designed for dedicated tasks to increase functionality and reliability.
In autonomous vehicles dozens of interconnected Electronic Control Units (ECUs) process data from radar, camera, and LiDAR sensors in real time to make split-second driving decisions and execute safety maneuvers without latency.
Examples include: Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (**ADAS**)
