## 2. **INTRODUCTION TO CMCM**

### A. **A Brief History of CMCM by the prophet/founder**

"I was at that time a student at Oyo State College of Art and Science (ISCAS). Ile-ife and a Muslim. I was with friends at home after school on this faithful day in late 1987, when I saw an open vision of heaven. Suddenly the weather changed as if it'll rain. I was was hearing heavy noises thundering the heavens were opened and there was a fire from heaven touching the ground running and another fire dropped from heaven ran after the first fire to overtake the first fire. At the point the second fire is about overtaking the first one I heard a thundering voice of the lord Jesus Christ say to me "Behold you have been giving Peter's power to command and take charge till I return." The power of the sound I heard The After which. the Lord Jesus Christ came down riding on an immaculate white horse and handed over a white horse and a Bible to me.

By this visitation I was shocked, bewildered, and laid helpless on the ground, suddenly I realized I have been blinded by the divine lights. I, indeed, lost my sight temporarily. It was a real baptism of fire and my life was never to be the same again.

This encounter was followed by a period of utter confusion. And all attempts by my parent to help me regain my sight proved abortive. Seven months later I regained my sight after I had given my life to the Lord Jesus Christ in submission to both the will and the call of God for my life.

On 13th march 1989, God directed me to leave ile-ife for Akure and in June 1989 the Ministry started as a family fellowship in a private car Garage in Akure. God added to the family daily and within a space of five months, an end-time Church: the Christ Miracle Church Mission (CMCM worldwide) sprang up at 36, Ifelodun Street, Oke-Ijebu, Akure and Ondo State, Nigeria.
